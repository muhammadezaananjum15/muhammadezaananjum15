## Who I Am

I'm a Software Engineering student based in Pakistan, focused on building the part of the web that people actually see and interact with. My work lives at the intersection of clean code and thoughtful design I care about interfaces that look good and feel natural to use.

Right now I'm deepening my understanding of advanced JavaScript, but my day to day is all about turning ideas into polished, responsive UIs. I believe consistency is the most underrated skill in development, and I try to show up and build something every single day.
